# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Trainer Lens to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone who uses or contributes to it, whatever their age, background, experience, identity or skill level.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or to Android.
- Give and accept feedback kindly. Criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intentions, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of spam or advertising.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone's private information (including exact locations in logs and screenshots) without their permission.
- Requests for, or sharing of, cheats, spoofing, bots or other ways to break the game's rules. Trainer Lens is a read-only helper and this project is not the place for them.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.
To report a problem, open a private report via the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** page or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
